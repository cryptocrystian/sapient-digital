## 1. The metaphor

White light enters a prism and leaves as a structured spectrum. One signal becomes four coherent emissions — all in phase, all measurable, all pointing the same direction.

That is what Sapient Digital does.

## 2. The problem · Fractured light

Growth-stage brands scatter one message across siloed agencies — PR here, content there, SEO somewhere else, video forgotten. The result is **fractured light**: budget spent, nothing coherent emitted. Buyers see noise. Google indexes somebody else. AI engines cite your competitor.

## 3. The solution · The Prism

Sapient Digital *is* the prism. We take one brand signal and refract it into four wavelengths that reinforce each other. Each wavelength does its own work; all four stay in phase.

## 4. The orchestration layer · PrismOS

PrismOS is the proprietary attribution and triggering layer that holds the four wavelengths in phase. It measures how one input creates output across every surface — a press placement into an AI citation into an organic rank into a video embed — and triggers the next action automatically. No black box. No fragmentation. One signal, one measurable spectrum.

## 5. The four wavelengths

| Wavelength | Service | What it does in the prism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Beam** | Earned media (PR) | The first strike. Pitches, tier-1 placements, journalist relationships. The signal that enters the prism. |
| **The Medium** | Editorial content | The substrate the beam passes through. Originals, bylines, research. Where authority is composed. |
| **The Refraction** | SEO + AEO | The structural bend. Technical SEO + answer-engine optimization. Where the signal is organized for every reader — human, Google, Perplexity, ChatGPT. |
| **The Spectrum** | Video | The full emission. Color + motion + reach. The one surface that already contains the other three. |

## 10. Visual direction

- **Signature mark.** A bespoke hand-drawn prism — triangular, isometric, varied line weight — with four coloured rays splitting out from the far face. "SAPIENT PRISM" embossed on one plane. This mark is the hero.
- **Color system.** The four wavelengths ARE the brand palette:
  - **The Beam** · Vermillion `#FF4F1A`
  - **The Medium** · Amber `#F59E0B`
  - **The Refraction** · Citron `#E8FF3A`
  - **The Spectrum** · Forest-Cyan `#1F7A5A` (or deep teal — tuned on build)

  Neutrals from the approved Signal Code palette restored: canvas `#F6F3EE`, paper `#FDFBF6`, ink `#0A0A08`, body, muted, rule, forest. One confident colour per screen; the full spectrum only in the hero and PrismOS moments.
- **Type stance.** Large confident sans display at TripleDart-adjacent scale, Inter body, JetBrains Mono for metadata. One restrained italic serif for pull quotes.
- **Interactive moments we actually build:** the hero prism (hover-reactive, each ray lights up), and the PrismOS attribution map (click to trace a single placement through the four wavelengths over time).
