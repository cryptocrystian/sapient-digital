# SAPIENT DIGITAL — PRODUCT CONSTITUTION
**Status:** CANONICAL · **Authority:** Strategic decisions, product scope, positioning
**Last Updated:** 2026-05-11

---

## Mission

Sapient Digital is an AI-native visibility agency that engineers category authority for
B2B and considered-purchase brands through three integrated, compounding disciplines:
earned media, content, and AI presence. Video production is the amplification layer.

We do not replace PR. We redefine what visibility can deliver.

---

## Non-Negotiable Truths

1. **Sapient is an agency first.** We are not a SaaS company, a tool reseller, or an AI
   wrapper. We are accountable for client outcomes, not software features.

2. **The intelligence is invisible.** Clients experience expertise. They do not experience
   automation. No tool names, no algorithm references, no "our AI" language in
   client-facing surfaces.

3. **The team gets the credit.** Every automated action is attributed to the team in
   client-facing copy. The automation is infrastructure; the strategy, judgment, and
   curation is the service.

4. **Outcomes over deliverables.** We do not sell coverage reports. We sell competitive
   intelligence, pipeline attribution, and market positioning. If a deliverable cannot
   be traced to a business outcome, it doesn't belong in our service model.

5. **Quality is the moat.** The barrier to competing with Sapient is not the tools — it
   is the standard. Craft quality, strategic precision, and outcome accountability are
   non-negotiable.

6. **Integration is the product.** The three Signal Engine pillars are architecturally
   inseparable. No client runs fewer than all three. Selling two pillars is selling a
   broken engine. Video is additive to the system, not a substitute for any pillar.

## What Sapient Digital Is

### The Signal Engine™ — Three Integrated Pillars

**PR & Media Relations** — Tier 1 placements in the publications target buyers actually
read. Pitching built on real journalist intelligence, not spray-and-pray distribution.
Placement rates that make traditional agencies look inefficient. Every placement feeds
content authority and AI citation rank.

**Content & Thought Leadership** — Executive positioning, ghostwriting, and editorial
strategy that builds authority over time. Content engineered to generate citations in
AI systems, not just traffic. The brief, the draft, the publish, and the measurement —
all integrated. Content output includes anchor pieces, executive LinkedIn programs, and
social/derivative layers.

**AI Presence & AEO** — Tracking and growing how often AI models cite a client's brand
in response to relevant queries. The visibility metric most agencies don't measure yet.
Structured data, entity reinforcement, citation monitoring across ChatGPT, Perplexity,
Gemini, and Claude. AI citations compound from PR and content authority — the pillars
feed each other by design.

### The Video Production Layer

**Video Content Stack** — AI-native production pipeline covering the full format taxonomy
that B2B buyers expect: hero/long-form content (2–5 min), executive thought leadership
videos (60–120s), short-form derivatives (15–45s), and Video Press Releases. At Dominate,
a complete monthly program produces 28+ video pieces — the cadence required for
consistent B2B presence (4 shorts/week minimum benchmark). Video is a production
infrastructure layer, not a Signal Engine pillar. It amplifies the three pillars but
does not replace any of them.

**Executive Avatar Program** — Scalable executive presence via AI avatar: voice clone +
likeness built once, consistent weekly video output without scheduling constraints.
Available as an add-on to any tier or video module.

## The Client Profile

**Target:** B2B and considered-purchase brands where buyers research, compare, and
evaluate over time before making a decision. The buying behavior — not the end customer
type — defines fit.

**Primary:** B2B companies with $10M–$500M in revenue. Verticals with natural affinity:
B2B SaaS and technology, industrial and manufacturing, professional services, healthcare
technology, energy and infrastructure.

**Considered-purchase B2C (qualifies under same behavioral profile):** Premium financial
services, healthcare and health technology, luxury real estate, high-end wellness and
professional services sold to individuals. These buyers research like B2B buyers — the
Signal Engine methodology applies without modification.

**Excluded:** Mass-market consumer brands, D2C e-commerce, and lifestyle brands where
impulse and emotion dominate the purchase cycle.

**Buyer:** CMO, VP Marketing, VP Communications, or CEO at companies where the
communications function is under-resourced relative to its strategic importance.

**Indicators a prospect is right for Sapient:**
- Currently spending $15k–$80k/year on PR, content, or marketing with mixed results
- Has real news — product launches, executive hires, research, partnerships — but no
  systematic way to amplify it
- Cares about AI citation visibility (or would care once they understand it)
- Has a video story to tell but lacks the infrastructure to produce it affordably
- Wants measurable outcomes tied to pipeline, not activity reports

## Pricing Philosophy

### Published pricing: "Starting at" model (not full feature grids)

The website pricing page displays tier names, starting prices, and a one-line
positioning statement per tier. No feature grids. The CTA is "Get your custom scope"
— framing discovery as customization, not sales.

Header copy rationale: Every program is scoped to the client's market, competitive
position, and velocity goals. Starting prices reflect the Signal Engine core.
The discovery session produces the exact deliverable mix.

**Why this approach:** Sophisticated CMO/VP Marketing buyers at $10M–$500M companies
expect to know ballpark investment before booking a call. "Contact for pricing" creates
friction and signals lack of confidence. Full feature grids invite line-item objections
and position the service as a commodity. "Starting at" anchors the conversation,
self-qualifies on budget, and positions the discovery call as value-adding rather than
a sales pitch.

## The Delivery Model

Sapient operates a **managed intelligence delivery** model:

1. **Signal detection** (continuous) — monitoring for opportunities, threats, and timing
   across client categories
2. **Action surfacing** (daily) — recommended actions prioritized by expected impact
3. **Human judgment** (account lead) — approving, delegating, scheduling, or dismissing
4. **Specialist execution** — pitching, drafting, producing, publishing
5. **Outcome measurement** (monthly/quarterly) — coverage velocity, AI citation rank,
   content performance, video performance, pipeline attribution

The client experiences steps 3 and 5 directly. Steps 1, 2, and 4 are infrastructure.

## Success Definition

**For a client:** They are consistently cited in Tier 1 publications in their category.
Their executives are recognized as thought leaders. Their AI presence rank is improving.
Their video content runs at a cadence that maintains consistent buyer-facing presence.
Their competitors are responding to them, not the other way around.

**For Sapient:** A portfolio where the majority of clients are in the "Competitive" or
"Dominant" status band on their visibility metrics. Retention above 18 months average.
Net revenue expansion from clients growing their retainer. Case study proof of pipeline
attribution connecting Signal Engine activity to closed revenue.

**For the agency:** A delivery model so efficient that account leads spend their time on
strategy and relationships — not formatting reports, chasing approvals, or manually
logging coverage.
